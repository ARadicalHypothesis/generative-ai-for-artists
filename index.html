Sustainable Generative AI for artists

An open, critical toolkit for making art with generative AI: open-source models, workflows that run on your own machine, data with consent, and an honest account of the costs.

Open first

Where an open-source tool or openly licensed model can do the job, we learn that one. Closed services are studied as objects of critique, not defaults.

Local and accountable

Run models on your own GPU. You know what runs, your material stays with you, and you can see the energy it uses.

Data with consent

Prefer models built on public-domain, licensed or opt-in material, and build your own datasets from work you have the right to use.

Why it matters

415 TWh

Electricity used by data centres worldwide in 2024, about 1.5% of the global total, projected to more than double by 2030 as AI grows.

International Energy Agency, Energy and AI (2025)

≈ 1 phone charge

Energy used for a single image by the least efficient model measured, SDXL. Image generation was the most energy-intensive of the ten tasks studied.

Luccioni, Jernite and Strubell, Power Hungry Processing (2023)

5 billion

Course history

Developed from AI for Artists, a graduate course Mattia Casalegno has taught in the Department of Digital Arts at Pratt Institute since 2023 (most recently Spring 2026), and from the public Generative AI workshop series he led at Pratt in Fall 2024 (3D form, game design, animation).

This open edition, published in October 2026, reorganises that material around open-source, locally run and accountable tools, and adds a critical frame on data, labour and energy. Related open resources: TouchDesigner for Artists and Tangible Feelings: EEG for the Arts.
