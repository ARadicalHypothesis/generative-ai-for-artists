# Projects

<p class="lede">The exercises follow the modules. Each one is small enough for a week, and each asks for documentation of process, not just results, because the process is where the learning is.</p>

## Exercises

| # | Exercise | Module |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two datasets.** Build one style or character dataset (20–40 images) and one GAN dataset (200+ images), both from material you have the right to use, each with a datasheet. | [02 Data and consent](modules/02-data-and-consent.md) |
| 2 | **Image to image with your own LoRA.** At least five stylised images, with every setting recorded. | [03 Images in ComfyUI](modules/03-images-in-comfyui.md) |
| 3 | **Train one LoRA** on your own work, test the epochs side by side, and show where it breaks. | [04 Training a LoRA](modules/04-training-a-lora.md) |
| 4 | **One brief, two systems.** The same 30-second brief made with an open local model and a closed platform, compared on control, cost, energy and provenance. | [05 Video and animation](modules/05-video-and-animation.md) |
| 5 | **Experimental motion.** Time-to-Move, a diffusion-rendered 3D scene, or a live StreamDiffusion patch in TouchDesigner. | [05 Video and animation](modules/05-video-and-animation.md) |
| 6 | **An asset you could fabricate or place.** 3D objects or a character with an open model, or a Gaussian-splat capture, cleaned in Blender or SuperSplat. | [06 3D and space](modules/06-3d-and-space.md) |
| 7 | **A soundtrack with provenance.** Score your video with open models and your own recordings, with full credits. | [07 Sound and voice](modules/07-sound-and-voice.md) |

For every exercise: a few slides on the process (inputs, model and settings, what failed, what you changed), the outputs, and the project checklist from [module 09](modules/09-sustainable-practice.md).

## Final project

A new piece or a new workflow: an installation, a video, a performance, an interactive experience, a tool. It should take a position on the questions this toolkit raises, not only use the tools.

**Suggested schedule (6 weeks):**

1. **Proposal.** A one-paragraph description; 2–3 images, renderings or sketches; a technical drawing or a flow chart of your pipeline; a timeline with milestones. Name the models you'll use and their licences, and where your data comes from.
2. Work in class.
3. **Milestone 1:** class review.
4. Work in class.
5. **Milestone 2:** class review.
6. **Final critique**, with documentation due within the week.

## Documentation standards

Documentation counts as much as the work. It is what curators, juries and future employers see.

**Video** (2–3 minutes; 16:9; 1920×1080; 30 fps; H.264 MP4; audio 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo, peaking between −6 and −1 dB):
- Tell the story of the project, covering its look, concept and engineering, with voice-over or captions.
- Include the title, your name, the year, and credits for the models, datasets and tools used.
- Optional: a vertical cut for social media (9:16, 1080×1920, up to 1 minute). Plan those shots in advance.

**Stills** (5 images):
- Shoot RAW, light the space, and cut the clutter.
- Show the whole work in its setting, people interacting with it, and details.
- Retouch for white balance, levels and perspective.
- Keep four versions of each image: RAW original, working PSD, an uncompressed TIFF at 5,000 px or more on the long side (for print), and a JPEG of about 2,000 px (for the web).

**PDF booklet:**
- Name, title, medium, dimensions or running time, year.
- A few paragraphs on the concept and intentions.
- Process: sketches, workflow diagrams, failed attempts, final results.
- A short **provenance section**: models, licences, datasets and their sources, and a rough energy estimate.
